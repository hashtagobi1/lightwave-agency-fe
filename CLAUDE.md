# Working on this repo

Read [DESIGN_BRIEF.md](./DESIGN_BRIEF.md) before writing or editing any
user-facing copy (React components, page metadata, or Sanity content that
renders on the site). Two rules from it worth restating up front:

1. This site exists to make LightWave money — brand deals, label budgets,
   client bookings. Write and design for that, not for its own sake.
2. Never use em dashes (—) in front-end copy. Use a comma, colon, period,
   or restructure the sentence instead.
