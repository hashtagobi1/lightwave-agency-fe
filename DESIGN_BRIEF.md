# LightWave Design Brief

Reference this before writing or editing anything user-facing on this site.

## Purpose

This site is a commercial tool, not a portfolio for its own sake. Its job is
to generate income for LightWave: brand deals, label budgets, event
sponsorships, and new client bookings. Every page a prospective client or
partner lands on (case studies, event recaps, the homepage) should read like
it was built to close deals, not like a personal blog. When in doubt between
"looks artistic" and "makes a brand/label/client want to pay LightWave to run
their next project," choose the latter.

## Voice & style rules

- **Never use em dashes (—) in front-end copy.** Rewrite with a comma,
  colon, period, or a restructured sentence instead. This applies to
  anything a visitor reads: page copy, metadata/titles, button labels,
  and content authored in Sanity (project/event descriptions, recaps,
  bios) that flows onto the site. Code comments are exempt.
  - Bad: `A tournament — 16 teams, four courts — capped off with a live set.`
  - Good: `A tournament: 16 teams, four courts, capped off with a live set.`
- Keep copy concrete and results-oriented (numbers, named partners, named
  venues) over vague enthusiasm ("amazing", "incredible") — specifics are
  what make a case study or event recap persuasive to a prospective client.

## Adding to this brief

When the user gives a new standing style/brand rule for the site, add it
here so it persists across sessions, rather than only applying it once.
